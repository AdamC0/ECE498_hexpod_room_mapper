AUTONOMOUS OBJECT HANDLING:
  By default, a tentative object does not stop mapping. It is confirmed after
  three distinct settled captures and may remain pending for up to five
  minutes. An immutable synchronized RGB/depth/TF snapshot may finish after
  the next route begins. Legacy object_focus is opt-in; when enabled, it stops
  for confirmation, two misses, or the 15 s limit before replanning.

BLOCKED ROUTES:
  The controller first stops. It rotates toward an open side when the 0.30 m
  swept footprint is clear. If rotation is constrained and rear LiDAR has at
  least lidar_stop + 0.20 m, it reverses briefly. It holds if neither is safe.
